A Promise Kept to Family

Founded during the 2025–2026 academic year at Northern Valley Regional High School at Demarest, LEAN sprouted not from a textbook, but from Sunday dining room tables, bedside quiet hours, and handwritten memory notebooks.

LEAN NV Demarest school Bergen County, NJ
psychology_alt The Story Behind The Care

Rachel’s journey began witnessing her paternal grandfather navigate Alzheimer’s disease across multiple gentle yet painful years. Later, when her maternal grandmother began facing identical neurological obstacles, the reality settled in: Alzheimer’s touches whole families, spanning generations.

Rather than feeling helpless against an unrelenting diagnosis, Rachel rallied classmates, faculty, and local businesses to construct a forum where young people learn, listen, and tangibly fund medical research alongside the Alzheimer’s Association.

What Is Alzheimer’s?

A progressive brain disorder, not a normal part of aging.

Alzheimer’s is the most common cause of dementia—accounting for 60% to 80% of dementia cases. It progressively destroys memory, reasoning, and communicative ability through the microscopic buildup of beta-amyloid plaques and tau tangles within neural pathways.

Accounts for majority of dementia diagnoses: 60–80%

02 • Early Signs

Signs & Symptoms

Distinguishing natural forgetfulness from disease signs.

  • Disruptive memory loss affecting daily calendar routines
  • Challenges planning, solving familiar number puzzles
  • Confusion with timeframes, seasons, or known places
  • Misplacing items and losing ability to retrace steps

Risk Factors

Genetics, biology & lifestyle balance.

Cardiovascular Health Heart-head link: hypertension & arterial vascular health.

04 • Medicine

Treatment & Care

Modern breakthroughs & symptom mitigation.

While a definitive cure remains the ultimate summit, newly FDA-approved anti-amyloid monoclonal antibodies slow cognitive decline during early stages. Complementary therapies prioritize sleep, socialization, and memory therapy.

05 • Compassion

Supporting Families

Patience, dignity & essential caregiver respite.

Supporting someone means honoring the individual behind the symptoms. Speak calmly, validate emotional states rather than arguing logic, and create supportive environments with predictable daily comfort.
