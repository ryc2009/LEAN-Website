Let’s End Alzheimer’s Now.

Students turning compassion into action.

LEAN is a student-led club at Northern Valley Demarest High School dedicated to raising awareness about Alzheimer's disease. Through fundraising, community events, and advocacy, we hope to make a meaningful difference for individuals and families affected by Alzheimer's.

Explore Our Story Support the Fight

Founded and run by NVD high school students dedicated to memory care.

Our Mission

Our mission is to unite students and our community in the fight against Alzheimer’s. By educating others, raising funds, and creating opportunities to get involved, LEAN works to support families today while contributing to the search for better treatments and prevention.
