Community Momentum

Small actions in our hallways create ripples across Bergen County.

Alzheimer’s affects more than 6 million Americans and countless families right here in northern New Jersey. Through student leadership, our club creates spaces where grandparents are honored, caregivers are supported, and young people lead with empathy.
