Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my contribution tax-deductible? help_outline

Yes, 100%. Because you donate directly through the Alzheimer’s Association’s encrypted server (a registered 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization), all donations qualify for federal and state tax deductions as permitted by law.

Can I dedicate my gift in memory or tribute of someone? favorite

Absolutely. On the official ALZ donation checkout screen, check the box labeled “I want to donate in honor or memory of someone.” You can enter their name, leave a tribute message, and specify an email or physical address to notify their family.

How does LEAN track donations if payment happens externally? insights

Our custom link routes directly to Rachel Weinberg’s official Chapter Leader profile within the Alzheimer’s Association fundraising network. The ALZ back-office automatically attributes each transaction to our Northern Valley Regional High School at Demarest student total.

Can I donate via check or stock transfer? account_balance

Yes. The Alzheimer’s Association accepts physical checks, donor-advised funds (DAF), and securities. Please reach out directly to our student leadership team at nvdleanclub@gmail.com and we will furnish the exact ALZ memo lines and physical deposit instructions.
