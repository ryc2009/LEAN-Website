Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Donations are processed by the Alzheimer’s Association, a registered 501(c)(3), and qualify as permitted by law.

Can I dedicate my gift?

Yes. The official checkout lets you donate in honor or memory of someone and send a notification.

Does my donation count toward LEAN’s goal?

Yes. The campaign link automatically attributes the transaction to our student fundraising total.

Can I donate by check or another method?

Contact our student leadership team for the correct Alzheimer’s Association instructions.
